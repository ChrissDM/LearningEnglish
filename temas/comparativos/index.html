Guía rápida de reglas

Comparativo: adjetivo + than → She is taller than me.

Superlativo: the + adjetivo + in / of → the best in the class · the best day of my life.

Igualdad: as ... as → He is as tall as his father.

Intensificar: much / a lot / far / a bit / a little + comparativo. Nunca very.
